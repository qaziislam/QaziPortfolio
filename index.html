OKLAHOMA HISTORY / COMMUNITY RECORD

Two years of production to document an under-told chapter of Muslim life in Oklahoma.

IMAN: Two Journeys of Faith, Rooted in Oklahoma's Heartland follows two American Muslim women and places their stories inside Oklahoma's social and civic timeline. The project was designed as both film and public-education tool.

The Oklahoma Historical Society hosted a public screening and panel in Oklahoma City on April 10, 2025, framing the film inside statewide historical dialogue.
